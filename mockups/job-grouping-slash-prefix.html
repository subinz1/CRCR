Slash-Prefix Job Grouping

Design idea: group CRCR job rows by the segment before the first / in job_name, so related suites collapse under a shared header on the per-repo / PR dashboard.

Partners already use slash-delimited names (e.g. upstream-tests / NeuralNet, linux-rhel9.6-spyre-py3.12 / test (cpu)). Grouping on the left-hand prefix keeps matrix density manageable without changing the callback schema.

Rule: split on the first " / " (space-slash-space) when present; otherwise treat the full job_name as its own group. Aggregated chips on the group header roll up child conclusions for at-a-glance scanning.
